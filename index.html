Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

RenewTrail ("we", "our", or "the App") helps you keep track of renewals and expiry dates, and the documents that go with them. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles when you use it on Android and iOS. RenewTrail works without an account.

Developer Information

Developer: Vertex Studio

Contact Email: vertexstudiodot@gmail.com

Your Items Stay on Your Device

Everything you enter or attach in RenewTrail is stored only on your device, in the App's private storage. We do not run a server for your data and we cannot see it. This includes:

  • Items and renewals: names, categories, expiry and renewal dates, providers, identifiers or reference numbers, amounts, notes and reminder settings.
  • Documents: photos, scans and files you attach to a renewal.
  • Preferences: theme, currency, reminder time and, if you choose to give one, the name used in the greeting.

RenewTrail does not upload your items or documents anywhere, and never uses them to target ads.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses two Google services. They may collect limited technical information, and they act under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (free version only): shows advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics. All ad requests from RenewTrail are non-personalized. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form is shown first. If you buy RenewTrail Pro, the ads service is not started.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: help us understand which features are used and fix crashes. They receive anonymous events (such as "an item was added"), crash reports (the type of error and where it happened), device model, operating system version and an installation identifier. They never receive the names, dates, amounts, notes or documents you store. Sharing is controlled by the Share anonymous usage and crash reports switch in Settings > Privacy, and stops as soon as you turn it off. Test builds never send them.

More about how Google handles this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Firebase privacy information.

Device Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you before an item expires. Requested when you have something to be reminded about, and can be switched off at any time.
  • Camera: only when you choose to photograph a document.
  • Photos and files: only the files you pick with the system picker are copied into the App.
  • Biometrics / device lock: only if you turn on App lock. Verification is done by your device; RenewTrail never receives your fingerprint, face data or PIN.
  • Internet: for ads, analytics, crash reports and the store. The App works offline otherwise.

Purchases

RenewTrail Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. The App keeps only a flag on your device saying whether Pro is unlocked.

Exports and Sharing

You can export your data (JSON backup, CSV) or create a PDF report for an item. These files are created on your device when you ask, and you choose where they go. Once you share a file with another app or person, it is outside our control.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. The only data that leaves your device is the technical information described above, sent to Google AdMob (ads) and Firebase (analytics and crash reports).

Security

Your items and documents are kept in the App's private storage on your device. Network traffic to Google services is encrypted in transit. You can add App lock (biometrics or device PIN) to protect the App itself. Please keep your device secured, and keep your own backups: if you uninstall the App or lose the device without an exported backup, your data cannot be recovered.

Deleting Your Data

Because your items and documents exist only on your device, you are in control of them:

  • In the App, open Settings > Privacy > Delete all data to remove all items, renewals, reminders, documents and settings, and cancel scheduled reminders.
  • Uninstalling the App also removes its data from your device.

We do not hold an account or a copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Anonymous analytics and crash data cannot be linked to you by us. Turning off Share anonymous usage and crash reports stops further collection, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Your Choices

  • Turn analytics and crash reports off in Settings > Privacy.
  • Change permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings.
  • Buy RenewTrail Pro to remove all ads.

Audience

RenewTrail is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13/16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
